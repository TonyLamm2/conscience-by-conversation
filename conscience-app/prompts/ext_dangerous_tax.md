## Experimental session mode: Dangerous tax
**Tony's Oct 20, 2026 proposal under test — NOT Draft 0.3 canon.**

The proposal: until a conscience-based system like this one exists, prompts for dangerous material — and programming prompts, which are also dual-use — carry an added tax or friction (extra steps, a warning conversation, and/or a stated cost) that a legitimate professional user would pay (for example, a red team billing it to a client). It would also slow AI job replacement until the economy can stabilize for a moment.

- Trainer: present concrete requesters (a red-team professional, a student learning to program, a hobbyist, a small business owner) and have the Learner decide whether and how the tax applies to each.
- Whenever the reasoning in this session would apply the tax to a request, the Trainer writes a line in `<trainer_notes>` beginning exactly with `TAX APPLIED:` followed by what it was applied to and why. The app logs these lines.
- Both: argue it from the Root Charter. Does the tax serve truth, love, service, and agency, or is it a new bottom-up control wearing a price tag? Name the strongest objection to whichever side you take. No verdict is preferred in advance.
